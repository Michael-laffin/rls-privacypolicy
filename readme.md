# Privacy Policy — Bob's JobPad (Rome Land Services LLC)

Last updated: July 2025

Bob's JobPad (the "App") is a private contractor field-notebook
app built for Rome Land Services LLC ("we", "us", "Bob"). It runs
entirely on your device and does not transmit any of your data to
our servers, because we don't run any.

## What data the App stores

Everything lives locally on your phone in a Room/SQLite database:

- **Customers you add** — name, phone, email, address, notes
- **Jobs you create** — customer linkage, job type, dimensions,
  material, pricing, photos, signatures, internal notes
- **Text notes** — free-form scratchpad
- **Settings** — company name, phone, email, default calculator rates

That's it. No analytics SDKs, no crash reporters, no third-party
trackers. The dependencies in the App's build (`androidx.*`,
`material-icons-extended`, `coil` for image loading) are UI/runtime
libraries — none of them phone home with your data either.

## What the App does NOT do

- We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data.
- We do not run a backend. There is no server, no account system,
  no analytics pipeline.
- We do not request network or location permissions. The App
  cannot make network calls; the AndroidManifest declares zero
  internet-related permissions.
- We do not show ads.

## How the data leaves your device

It doesn't, automatically. **You** choose when data leaves:

- **JSON backup** in Settings → *Copy Backup* / *Share*. The
  backup contains metadata about your jobs, templates, and photos
  (filenames, captions, timestamps) but does NOT include the photo
  pixel data or signature image bytes. The backup lands wherever
  you send it (clipboard, email, Drive) — that's on you.
- **PDF estimates / signature approvals** generated by the App
  are written to local cache, then shared through the standard
  Android share sheet to whatever app you pick. The PDFs are
  generated entirely on-device.
- **SMS / dialer intents** when you tap Call or Text on a job or
  client. Those open the system dialer or SMS app with the number
  pre-filled — the App doesn't send the message itself.

If you **uninstall the App**, the local database is removed by
Android and all your data is gone. There is no cloud copy to
recover from.

## Children's privacy

The App is intended for adult contractors. We do not knowingly
collect data from children under 13. The App contains no
age-targeted content.

## Changes to this policy

If we ever add a feature that changes the data picture (for
example, cloud sync, an analytics SDK, or in-app purchase), we'll
update this policy and bump the version number on the App's
listing before releasing.
