# Privacy Policy — JobPad (Rome Land Services LLC)

Last updated: October 5, 2026

JobPad is a contractor field notebook for Rome Land Services LLC. It saves information locally on the Android device. Starting with version 1.0.12, you can enable automatic backup to the business Google Drive account, romelandservices@gmail.com. There is no separate JobPad server, advertising, analytics service, or sale of user data.

## Information saved by JobPad

JobPad stores the information you enter: jobs and customer contact information; addresses and job locations entered in the notebook; job details, materials and loads, prices and estimated time; notes; estimates, expenses, text templates, status records and approvals; photos and their captions; uploaded documents; signatures; generated PDF reports; and saved business settings and calculator rates. The database and attachment copies remain on the device. Choosing a document copies it into JobPad's private storage.

## Optional automatic Google Drive backup

Backup is off until you tap **Connect Google Drive** in Settings, review the in-app explanation, and authorize JobPad through Google. JobPad requests the limited **drive.file** permission to create and manage its own backup files and files you explicitly open with it. JobPad checks that the connected Drive account is romelandservices@gmail.com. It reads the account's email address for this check.

When enabled, saved notebook records and actual attachment files listed above are sent over HTTPS directly to Google Drive. Google processes and stores these copies under its own terms and privacy policy: [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). JobPad uses Google Play services for account authorization. It never asks for, embeds, or saves your Google password. Access tokens are used in memory for the requested Drive operations.

Backups appear in a **JobPad** folder. Each phone has a separate folder containing jobs, clients, general notes, settings and recovery backups. Each job's folder contains its details, notes, photos, documents, approvals and generated reports when available. A random identifier generated by JobPad distinguishes phones; JobPad does not use the phone's IMEI, contacts, location services, or an advertising identifier for this purpose. Separate phone notebooks do not merge or edit one another.

Changes made offline stay saved on the phone and wait for an internet connection. Android controls when background work can run. Settings displays the last complete backup and any connection or file problem. A complete backup is recorded only after the uploaded files are checked. Drive storage capacity and Google authorization limits can prevent a backup from finishing.

## Retention and controls

You can pause automatic backup in JobPad Settings. You can also revoke JobPad's authorization in your Google Account. Pausing or revoking access does not remove files already in Drive.

Deleting a job or attachment locally retains its previously backed-up files under the phone's **Archived** folder in Drive. Complete recovery snapshots are retained, and previous attachment versions may remain available. To remove those cloud copies, delete them using Google Drive; Google's Trash and retention rules apply. JobPad does not automatically empty Drive Trash or permanently delete your cloud backups.

Uninstalling JobPad removes its local database and app-owned attachment files. Google Drive copies already backed up remain in the business account. Version 1.0.12 provides complete data and file backup but does not include an in-app import/restore tool. Update an existing installation in place to preserve its local notebook.

## Other ways you choose to share data

**Copy Backup / Share** exports a limited JSON record through the clipboard or an app you select; this older manual export does not include actual photo or document files. **PDF sharing** sends an on-device report through Android's share sheet. **Call / Text** opens the phone's dialer or messaging app with the recipient number. The text composer is left blank, and JobPad does not send a call or message on your behalf. Choosing a camera or file picker is an explicit action; JobPad does not inspect unrelated files or other apps' messages.

## Children's privacy and changes

JobPad is intended for adult contractors and is not directed at children under 13. We update this policy and the in-app disclosures when data handling changes. Google authorization is requested in context before Drive backup begins.
