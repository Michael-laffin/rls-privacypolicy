JobPad

A private Android field notebook for Rome Land Services. Keep jobs, customers, notes, materials, estimates, photos, and documents together by job number.

Optional Google Drive backup saves each phone's notebook in a separate folder in the business Google account. Backup starts only after you connect that account in JobPad Settings and approve Google's permission request. Saved changes then upload automatically when an internet connection is available.

JobPad requests access only to Drive files it creates or that you explicitly open with it. There is no separate JobPad server, advertising, or sale of user data.

JobPad is distributed directly as a signed Android APK for the business. Update the existing installation in place to retain the phone's jobs. It does not require a Google Play testing track.
